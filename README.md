## Reflection
For this flat game I wanted to make it about my new public transport concept, an **infinite moving walkway of gradient speed.** Its like a moving walkway in an airport except as you move further out into the moving walkway, the walkway accelerates exponentially. A rider could get on or off at any point and control their own acceleration simply by moving in further or closer. The concept was that the player would move around in this space and see all types of people on the platform moving at different speeds. They can move forward and back to adjust their speed to move close to an NPC, where the NPC will share a bit about themselves and the world. Hopefully the fun or interesting mode of movement combined with an interesting world or characters would be enjoyable to interact with.

During the process I definitely got caught up in getting the shader and animation of the character to a point where I was happy with it, which left me with little time to work on NPCs and worldbuilding. I think the worldbuilding could have been very intriguing, but I did not flesh it out really, so all that is revealed is that there is a Government structure that regulates movement to be only on the walkway, the people on it do not know where it goes, and there are people who do not use the walkway (called "walkers", usually pejoratively). If i were to work on this project more it would be work on the world, because being intrigued in that is what will motivate the player to try to find more people to talk to.
